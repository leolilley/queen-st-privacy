Queen St Ads: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Queen St Ads is a private Meta app used to set up and manage advertising for the sale of a family home in Raetihi, New Zealand. It is run by Leo Lilley and is not a public service. Nobody signs in to it or installs it, other than the people running the ads.

What the app accesses

The app uses Meta's Marketing API to create and manage ads on the ad account and Facebook Page it has been authorised to use. It reads details of that ad account and Page, and the targeting options Meta makes available.

What the app does not do

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal information about people who see or click the ads. It does not use tracking pixels or lead forms.

Ads and enquiries

Ads shown on Facebook and Instagram are subject to Meta's own privacy policy. Ad links lead to the real estate listing on the Bayleys website, where that website's own privacy policy applies. Any enquiry made there is handled by the listing agent, not by this app.

Access tokens

The access token that lets the app work is kept privately by the people running the ads and is not shared. It can be removed at any time through Facebook's settings.

Contact and data deletion

The app holds no personal information about the public to delete. For any privacy question or request, email leo.lml.lilley@gmail.com.
